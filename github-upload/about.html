I'm Annie, a product designer and researcher based in Chicago, with a Cognitive Science degree from UCLA (Cum Laude, GPA 3.93) and a minor in Entrepreneurship. My path into design didn't start with pixels. It started in behavioral research labs, where I learned to ask the right questions before ever sketching a screen.

At UCLA Anderson School of Management, I designed research stimuli for Professors Franklin Shaddy and Hal Hershfield, translating behavioral economics questions into dozens of Qualtrics studies. That taught me how small design choices quietly shape the way people respond, decide, and behave, a lesson that now sits underneath every interface I design.

Today I'm an Analyst at Tegus by AlphaSense, where I've built a network of 280+ industry experts and facilitated 270+ research calls, sharpening how I turn ambiguous problems into structured, actionable insight. Along the way I've also built customer personas for boutique hotel owners, mapped go-to-market research for a CPG beverage brand, and helped a youth sports startup think through expansion.

When I'm not designing or researching, I'm probably baking something from scratch, planning the next trip, or cheering on the Bruins.
