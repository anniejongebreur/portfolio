I'm Annie, a consumer researcher based in Chicago, with a Cognitive Science degree from UCLA (Cum Laude, GPA 3.93) and a minor in Entrepreneurship. I'm fascinated by how people make decisions and why they choose what they do. That curiosity started in behavioral research labs, where I learned to ask the right questions before ever collecting a single response.

At UCLA Anderson School of Management, I designed research stimuli for Professors Franklin Shaddy and Hal Hershfield, translating behavioral economics questions into dozens of Qualtrics studies. That taught me how small choices in how a question or an option is framed quietly shape the way people respond, decide, and behave, a lesson that now sits underneath all of my research.

Today I'm an Analyst at Tegus by AlphaSense, where I've built a network of 280+ industry experts and facilitated 270+ research calls, sharpening how I turn ambiguous problems into structured, actionable insight. Along the way I've also built customer personas for boutique hotel owners, mapped go-to-market research for a CPG beverage brand, and helped a youth sports startup think through expansion.

I'm also developing a new interest in sensory science and food. I love baking, and I'm curious about how flavor, texture, and expectations shape what people enjoy and what they choose to eat. It feels like a natural next step for someone who studies consumer decisions and spends weekends baking from scratch.

I'm interested in pursuing further education to deepen my understanding of consumer decision making and sensory science. I want to study consumers from several perspectives: lifestyles, consumer behavior, product design, economics, and communication science.

When I'm not researching, I'm probably in the kitchen, planning the next trip, or cheering on the Bruins.
