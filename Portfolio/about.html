I'm Annie, an analyst based in Chicago, with a Cognitive Science degree from UCLA and a minor in Entrepreneurship. I'm fascinated by how people make decisions and why they choose what they do. That curiosity started in behavioral research labs, where I learned to ask the right questions before ever collecting a single response.

I'm interested in how environments shape decisions, the ethics of this science, sensory science and food, how experiences shape decisions, and when people act rationally and irrationally, among many other questions. My interests are broad, but they all center on decision making and consumer science. I love watching these ideas applied to industry and policy problems, and advocating for the best interests of consumers and people.

My goal is to pursue further education and become a researcher in consumer decision making and sensory science. I want to study consumers from several perspectives: lifestyles, consumer behavior, product design, economics, and communication science.

When I'm not working, I'm probably in the kitchen, planning the next trip, or cheering on the Bruins.
